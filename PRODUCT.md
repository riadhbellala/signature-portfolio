# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router (16), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4 with CSS-first `@theme` in `src/app/globals.css`, `motion` for the puzzle snap and reveals, `clsx`. Fonts via `next/font`. npm. No UI kit, no icon library.

## Users

Businesses in Oran and across Algeria looking for an agency to handle their image: shop owners needing a façade, signage or a mural; companies needing a website, a logo, an event stand or social media. They arrive from Instagram (@signature.alg) or a referral and want to see real work before calling.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio site for Signature creative co. (Signature Consulting), a 360° advertising and marketing agency in Oran. It shows the work across all trades and gets the visitor to contact the studio.

## Positioning

One studio that does both the digital (sites, social) and the physical (walls, paintings, façades, signage, stands). The puzzle campaign ("We just build") frames this: the agency assembles the pieces of a brand's presence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: Site web, Identité & logo, Murs (murals), Tableaux (paintings), Façade & enseigne, Stands & événements, Réseaux sociaux. Copy is French. Current step: design system and `/styleguide` only; pages follow one at a time after client confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

Script "Signature" logo in teal with "creative co." in ochre; never re-typeset or recoloured. Three colours only (cream, teal, ochre) with tonal steps. Poppins set heavy and tight. Devices from their posts: ochre offset outline, dashed looping ochre line, ochre rule under labels, ochre bullets, giant hairline script trace in the background, puzzle pieces. See `docs/brand-analysis.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

## Product Principles

1. Look like it came from the same studio as the posts.
2. Show the work; claims stay out until there is proof.
3. One idea per screen, left-aligned, generous paper.
4. The puzzle is the brand device and is used sparingly so it stays strong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for every text/background pair, visible focus rings, full keyboard use, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, usable at 360px.
